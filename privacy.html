On-device

What “local” actually means.

  • No file contents leave your Mac — with Ollama, documents are processed locally, not sent to any server.
  • No account — download and start without signing up. AI is bring-your-own-key.
  • Two optional exceptions, both your choice — web search works only if you add a Brave or Tavily key, and then sends just the search query. Accurate PowerPoint previews ask Keynote or PowerPoint on your own Mac to render the deck; nothing is uploaded.
