Why I built it

Finder is fine for browsing. Not for real file work.

When your day is projects, screenshots, PDFs, code, downloads and archives, the work spreads across too many windows and tools. Kommanda brings it back into one native Mac app — fast, keyboard-first, and careful with your files.

“Show me what you'll change, then change it.”

What to try first

Three things that show what it's for.

1. Copy a project without the junk

Right-click a project → Copy Without. No node_modules, no .env secrets, no build output — then paste it anywhere.

2. Find the space

Open Reclaim Space (⌘D) and see how much rebuildable junk is hiding across your Mac — 75+ GB on one developer's machine. Pro, and in the trial.

3. Work in two panes

F5 copies, F6 moves, Tab switches sides. Preview code, PDFs and slides without opening another app.

The assistant · Pro

It renamed the screenshots for this launch.

Select messy screenshots and ask Kommanda to name them by what each one shows. It drafts the names, shows you every change, and waits — apply all, review each, or cancel.
